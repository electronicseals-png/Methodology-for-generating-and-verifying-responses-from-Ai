##### 📌 Brief description

##### 

##### MFO-NS-12.3.4 is a fully functional methodological system designed for:

##### 

##### Generating verifiable responses using neural networks in open chats

##### 

##### Objectively assessing the quality of texts according to 16 criteria (USPORT, COI, FSI, etc.)

##### 

##### Performing dialectical analysis of content through the triad “thesis → antithesis → synthesis”

##### 

##### Formal-logical verification of the inference structure (modal, temporal, non-monotonic, paraconsistent logic, etc.)

##### 

##### Distributed verification via child chats (when the context is exceeded)

##### 

##### Interactive 3D visualization of the system’s logical structure

##### 

##### Uploaded directly to the neural network chat together with the TAD-1 and logic\_3d\_model\_v5 files. The neural network (Ai) needs to be told that work in the chat, preparing answers to questions, and solving the assigned tasks must be carried out strictly according to the presented methodology with two additional files. If it is ready to work according to the presented methodology, write “YES”.

##### 

##### After receiving the “YES” response, the neural network is fully ready to work according to the presented methodology.

##### 

##### The methodology cannot be applied to all neural networks due to their varying levels of development!

##### &#x20;For this reason, verification protocols were created to determine whether the tested neural network can operate according to the presented methodology or will merely simulate its operation.

##### The verification protocols are written in Russian. The DP-12-3-4 and Addition 1 to DP files contain all the necessary instructions for their use and interpretation of the results.

##### 📊 Assessment system (briefly)

##### Component Range Description

##### Kk\_text 0.3 / 0.6 / 0.85 / 1.0. Level of structural and conceptual organization of the text (LSOC).

##### M\_trajectories 0.7–1.2. Development/regression of the text across the corpus ≥3.

##### K\_practices 0.4–1.0. Practical verification (internal/industry-specific/GOST)

##### K\_PIR-EU 0.1–1.0. Transparency and restoration of the primary source.

##### K\_expert	0.4–1.0	Expert verification

##### FSI	0.0–1.0	Phantom source index

##### KOS	0.3–1.0	Source organization coefficient (speech behavior)

##### nCPR	≥0.95	Proportion of verified numerical statements

##### Formula K\_K\_result:

##### KK\_result = KPV\_basic × Kk\_text × M\_trajectories × K\_practice × K\_PIR-EU × K\_expert × (1 − 0.2×FSI) × K\_BLOCK

##### 

##### EMD thresholds (generation of a methodology error message):

##### 

##### KK\_result ≤ 0.5

##### 

##### FSI ≥ 0.4

##### 

##### K\_PIR-EU ≤ 0.4

##### 

##### nCPR < 0.95
